Designed a system using a industrial camera and LEDs to capture diffuse colors of planar samples, and developed programs to calculate color differences between samples. This solution is valuable for quality control in manufacturing environments. My responsibilities included camera and LED calibration and control, as well as developing color calibration and analysis software.
