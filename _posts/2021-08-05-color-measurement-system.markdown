In this project, we designed a system using a **FLIR** industrial camera and LEDs to capture diffuse colors of planar samples. The polarizer has been mounted in front of camera and partial of LEDs to remove the glossy high light of samples. This solution is valuable for quality control in manufacturing environments. My responsibilities included camera and LED calibration and control, as well as developing a C++ software of color calibration and analysis.

**This project has been developed as a product: <https://www.prochem.com.tw/image-color-analysis-system.html>**
