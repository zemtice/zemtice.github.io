Employed Generative Adversarial Networks (GAN) to synthesize texture images including wood, stone, and fabric. Utilized TensorFlow to build and train both GAN and super-resolution network models. The system serves the interior decoration industry by creating stylish elements for tiles and wallpaper, and can generate texture maps for application in 3D realistic rendering.
